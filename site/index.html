Why I built this

I draw a lot of diagrams while I build things, and most AI diagram tools hand me a PNG.

The moment one box is wrong, or I want to try a different shape for the system, I'm back to asking the AI again. That's backwards for design work. I don't want an architecture generated in one shot. I want to think my way into it.

AI is great at the draft: the obvious boxes and arrows, in seconds. After that the valuable part is mine. Dragging things around, noticing what's missing, deciding where a boundary should go. That's where the critical thinking happens, and it needs a canvas, not a picture.

How it works

The model says what. draftr decides where.

Coordinates are not the model's job. Your agent writes nodes and edges; draftr lays them out, draws them, checks them, and hands you a file.

01

Your agent describes the system

Nodes, edges, groups and the one thing that matters. No x, no y.

"nodes": [
  { "id": "order", "label": "Order Service",
    "highlight": true },
  { "id": "db", "label": "MongoDB",
    "kind": "database" }
],
"edges": [{ "from": "order", "to": "db",
             "label": "insert" }]
02

draftr lays it out and checks it

ELK layout with orthogonal routing and nested groups. An icon for every box, picked from its label. Twelve lint rules run before anyone looks at it.

03

You open it and keep going

Boxes, icons and labels move together, and arrows stay attached. Docs get an SVG that looks the same.

The checkout example, exactly as draftr draws it. Order Service is the highlighted node.

Details

Small things that save a round trip.

Lint before you look

Overlaps, labels spilling out of boxes, arrows through the database. Every finding names the element and the fix, so the agent can act on it without squinting at a PNG.

E [shape-overlap] auth and orders overlap by 50×20px
    fix: move orders at least 36px away
E [text-over-shape] "Auth service (JWT)" spills out by 71px
    fix: widen auth to ≥201px
W [arrow-through-text] a_orders_kafka runs through "order.created"

Icons from the label

Write "Postgres" and you get the elephant. 240 developer brands and 124 line icons, matched in English or Korean. Add a folder of your own logos and they win.

Mermaid and sequence diagrams

Paste the Mermaid that's already in your docs, or describe participants and messages. Columns are spaced so every message label fits.

Tidy a messy file

Point draftr at a hand-made .excalidraw. It recovers the boxes, labels and arrows and lays them out again.
